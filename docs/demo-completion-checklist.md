3. Borrower completes the fictional application; LO reviews and shares a sample quote.
4. Processor accepts the handoff, resolves mock document/condition work, and uses a private review or communication follow-up.
5. Staff record closing and funding; owner verifies the changed dashboard and financial view.
6. Show the partner milestone, past-client follow-up, and repeat-loan history.
7. Briefly show search/calendar, one recoverable exception, and the migration review/export path.

Prepare a concise presenter script with role-switch instructions, expected values, supported actions, and explicit mock boundaries. Time the narrated run; do not substitute an automated click-run duration for presentation duration.

## Blockers and completion decision

A blocker is any failure of the required checks, cross-tenant/role exposure, lost or duplicated work, incorrect totals, inability to complete the run, or misleading live-service behavior. Fix and retest blockers before completion. Cosmetic issues may remain only if documented with impact and they do not obstruct a required check or presentation.

Completion requires all D01–D15 marked Pass with evidence, no unresolved blockers, a reproducible starting state, and a recorded final candidate. Do not mark this checklist complete based solely on test count or previously deployed feature slices.
