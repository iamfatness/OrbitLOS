# Compliance implementation and release specification

Status: required implementation backlog; research explorer delivered, transaction enforcement not yet delivered. Baseline: 2026-11-10. Read [scope and limitations](README.md), [federal research](federal.md) and both state matrices first. This document specifies engineering behavior; it does not resolve a legal question by choosing the stricter-looking value.

## Architecture and records

The [Compliance Risk & Gaps specification](risk-and-gaps-spec.md) defines how these requirements become brokerage oversight, canonical findings, file-level remediation and protected-action decisions. Its [RG-0–RG-6 delivery plan](risk-and-gaps-plan.md) maps back to C01–C12 below. UWM implementation remains first; this is the next workstream. Required controls for any live UWM activity remain prerequisites to that activity.

Use the existing per-brokerage Durable Object and transactional SQLite boundaries for decisions and evidence. Keep public rule versions separate from tenant-specific approvals and loan facts. Every material decision must be reproducible from the facts and rule version at the time of the action.

| Record | Required fields and invariants |
| --- | --- |
| Rule version | Stable ID; jurisdiction and regulator; authority URL and section; archived source digest; retrieved/effective/end dates; amendment/supersession relationship; applicability predicate; required facts; trigger; calculation/calendar; exceptions; required evidence; reviewer and review date; implementation and test references. Draft/research rules cannot authorize actions. |
| Brokerage scope | Tenant; legal entity; business model; approved products and activities; applicable jurisdictions; license regimes and exemptions; lender responsibility matrix; compliance officer; approved rule-set versions; approval expiry/revalidation triggers. Default unknown, never all states enabled. |
| License evidence | Company/branch/person identifiers; regulator; license type; state; sponsorship/branch affiliation; status; effective/expiry dates; verification timestamp/source; exemption basis if reviewed. NMLS ID text alone is not eligibility. |
| Loan compliance facts | Property/borrower/activity locations; purpose and documented purpose evidence; occupancy; collateral and dwelling/lien/product classification; entity role; six-piece application event history where applicable; creditor and broker identities; applicant parties; current loan terms; fees and compensation; event timezone and calendar references. Unknown is a separate value, never false. |
| Consent | Tenant/person/channel/purpose/vendor/recipient scope; disclosure version and rendered text/hash; affirmative action and timestamp; address/number provenance; E-SIGN access demonstration where required; withdrawal/revocation and scope; suppression fan-out receipts. Do not infer consent from a CSV row or a generic checkbox. |
| Disclosure package | Rule/template/provider versions; immutable terms/fees snapshot; documents and hashes; required recipients and roles; issuance, delivery and receipt evidence; signature artifacts; revisions and superseded links; lender acknowledgments; corrected-package reason; independently calculated deadlines. Manual status is labeled as an attestation, not verified delivery. |
| Decision | Tenant/resource/action; authorized actor; input version/hash; evaluated rule versions; applicable/not-applicable/unresolved with reasons; obligations, deadline and owner; blockers and evidence references; decision timestamp. Only evaluate current facts within the mutation transaction. |
| Exception | Exact rule/action; legal authority permitting exception; reviewer role; rationale; evidence; scope and expiry. A user cannot override a statutory prohibition by acknowledging a warning. |
| Retention and holds | Record class; jurisdiction/product scope; trigger; applicable period; retention-until; custody agreement; legal hold; export history; destruction approval and receipts. Resolve overlapping duties and deletion requests; never globally delete all records after one duration. |

Use UTC instants plus explicit jurisdiction/recipient timezone and legal date. Version holiday calendars and distinguish general business days from rule-specific calendar definitions. Do not calculate statutory time from Orbit's existing Eastern-time CRM scheduling convention. A change in loan facts, state, owner/license, terms, fees, consent or source law invalidates dependent decisions and queues reevaluation.

## Server enforcement points

| Workflow | Required behavior | Existing integration surface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding/assignment | Resolve company, branch and MLO authority for the activity; block unknown/expired/suspended authority and unsupported scope; preserve licensing evidence | `brokerages.ts`, `loan-assignments.ts`, staff access and lead routing |
| Application intake | Record each legally relevant application element when received through any channel, not only at final submit; create applicable disclosure/notice obligations; do not wait for extra documents to start a clock | `applications.ts`, `urla.ts`, `lead-inbox.ts`, `commands.ts` |
| Credit order | Validate permissible purpose, required authorization and applicable notices before provider request; track provider reference; apply restricted data access | `mock-credit-aus.ts` and future live adapter |
| Pricing/fees/compensation | Determine legal/product applicability; assess fee timing, tolerances, high-cost thresholds and compensation restrictions using approved calculators; reject absent facts; retain offered and chosen terms | `quote-worksheet-store.ts`, `pricing-provider.ts`, `lender-export.ts` |
| Disclosure and signing | Approve recipient/package/version; issue through validated provider or capture independently verifiable lender evidence; reconcile duplicate/out-of-order callbacks; enforce applicable waiting/re-disclosure periods | `loan-processing.ts`, `commands.ts` manual disclosure branch; planned document/signature adapters |
| Outreach | Immediately before actual send/dial/export-to-dialer, check channel consent, suppression, DNC applicability/current screening, local time, frequency and state restrictions; reserve attempt atomically; record delivery/attempt receipts | `outreach-preferences.ts`, `communications.ts`, `call-campaigns.ts`, `campaign-follow-up-runs.ts`; future live sending adapters |
| Adverse action/incomplete/withdrawn | Classify event accurately; identify responsible entity, deadline, reason set and notice evidence; do not use ordinary pipeline disposition as proof of notice fulfillment | `commands.ts`, `loan-workflow.ts`, future notice service |
| Closing/funding | Require applicable verified package, fee/term decisions, waiting-period completion and lender evidence; allow recording an external historical event while separately flagging a violation—do not hide reality to preserve a green status | closing workbench and `loan-processing.ts` |
| Export/reporting | Separate HMDA, NMLS MCR and state report scopes; validate period/schema and accountable filer; preserve submission receipt and correction history; limit recipient data | `call-report.ts`, `lender-export.ts`, `pipeline-export.ts` |
| Deletion/security | Resolve retention/legal holds before document cleanup, replacement and tenant deletion; enforce least privilege and minimize partner views; support incident evidence and jurisdiction-specific notice workflow | `store.ts`, document cleanup/scanner, `workspace-access.ts`, `realtor-portal.ts` |

Run checks in server mutations and provider dispatch, not only in React. Cover Next local and Worker hosted service paths. Client-supplied approval state is untrusted. Use idempotency keys for provider events and append decision evidence in the same transaction as the authorized change; a stale snapshot must fail and request reevaluation. Avoid remote provider calls inside SQLite transactions; use an outbox/reservation and revalidate time-sensitive consent/eligibility at dispatch.

Priorities indicate sequencing, not permission to postpone an applicable requirement. C10 or C12 becomes P0 when a proposed launch depends on it. Manual controls can satisfy a release criterion only where legally permissible, named, evidenced, tested and reviewed; a blanket override is insufficient.

## Release evidence and ongoing ownership

For each enabled brokerage/state/product/activity, require: legal applicability sign-off; all required control implementations; negative-path test evidence; verified provider/lender responsibilities; operational owner and backup; retained sample artifacts; training and incident/complaint procedures; monitor and rollback plan. Obtain legal review of conflicting, stale or limited-retrieval sources. Mark each requirement independently as researched, legally reviewed, implemented, tested, operationally evidenced and release-approved. Never compress these into one “compliant” checkbox.

At this baseline **no jurisdiction receives release approval from the register**. Its coverage checks verify catalog integrity only. The implementation above must be completed and validated against the brokerage's actual approved scope before Orbit can claim to handle those duties in live workflows. Keep research and this backlog linked to the product delivery plan so future provider features cannot bypass compliance gates.


## Pilot loan retention

In **Compliance → Loan retention**, a current pilot owner can search loans by borrower, filter to active holds and open a loan. Counts cover checklist requests and distinct PDF references, including versions retained by an active hold. An owner can place or release a hold with a reference. The history records who acted and when. Loan officers and processors cannot access the inventory or change a hold.

An active hold blocks loan deletion and a sample reset that would remove the loan. It preserves existing and replacement checklist PDFs while normal loan work continues. Releasing a hold restores the normal cleanup rules for obsolete copies. The loan and current documents follow their existing lifecycle. Hold history survives release. The ordinary restore flow blocks active holds and checkpoints that would rewind hold history. Use the incident review for a workspace with an active hold. Keep this protection in active and rollback releases while any hold exists.

This pilot covers loan and checklist records. Policy periods, holds for other record types, archived-byte export, and review of production retention and disposition remain open.
