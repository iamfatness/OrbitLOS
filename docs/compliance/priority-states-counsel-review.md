# Priority-state compliance review

Michigan is the first production pilot. California, Florida, Texas, New York, Colorado and Illinois are the next release group. Use the [Michigan pilot review](michigan-pilot-counsel-review.md) for Michigan and complete a separate review below for each proposed legal entity, state and license path.

## Evidence and acceptance for each state

1. In **Compliance → Research & gaps → State rollout**, save the brokerage scope with the exact company and originator authority, branches and remote locations, product and lien types, broker and lender responsibilities, and dates. Download that version with its source snapshots. Start with the licensed broker activity proposed for release. Open separate scope reviews for other roles or authorities the entity wants to use.
2. For each applicable requirement, record the authority and effective period, who performs the duty, the event that starts the clock, approved exceptions, and the Orbit control or reviewed operating procedure. Assign an owner and review date to each missing item.
3. Rehearse an authorized and an unauthorized originator or office, an on-time and a late or missing disclosure, a regular and zero-activity reporting period, an amended filing, a published ad and an examination file. Attach the exact workpaper, receipt or fictional receipt, delivery evidence, and retained file versions. Use the [reporting calendar](state-rollout-criteria.md#reporting-calendar-for-the-priority-release-group) for dates and report-specific review questions.
4. Have the owner and counsel review the same saved scope and evidence. Capture the disposition, reviewer, date and reference for each requirement. Record the tested release and eligible workspace when deployment has been verified.

## California

- Identify whether the brokerage is acting under a DRE broker or corporation endorsement, California Financing Law authority, or a CRMLA license. Check matching originator authority, sponsorship, permissible counterparties and branch arrangements before allowing work. Rehearse a DRE-sponsored originator assigned to a DFPI entity and a company with multiple authorities. [DFPI originator FAQs](https://dfpi.ca.gov/regulated-industries/mortgage-loan-originators/mortgage-loan-originators-faqs/) · [DRE originator licensing](https://www.dre.ca.gov/licensees/mlolicense.html)
- For the DRE path, have counsel approve the Mortgage Loan Disclosure Statement trigger, applicable form and permitted federal disclosure treatment. Rehearse a written completed application, an obligation on the note before the ordinary delivery deadline, and a claim of substitute disclosures. Include the approved ad identifiers and records needed for a DRE examination. [California DRE real estate law](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/relaw/relaw.pdf) · [DRE advertising guidance](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re858.pdf)
- In a local pilot workspace, open **Loan → Disclosures → California mortgage disclosure evidence**. Record each borrower's delivery against the documented third business day and actual note obligation. The DRE statement route tracks signatures and the executed copy. The Loan Estimate substitute route tracks separate acknowledgment and California supplement evidence. Changing routes requires fresh borrower evidence. Have counsel confirm the calendar and product-specific form and reconcile §10240(c)'s threshold language with DRE guidance at exactly $30,000 for senior liens and $20,000 for junior liens. Counsel also needs to review negotiated-language duties, retained evidence and overlapping federal requirements. [DRE forms](https://www.dre.ca.gov/forms/MLB.html) · [DRE Loan Estimate advisory](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/adv/2015_2019/Advisory2015MLDS%20and%20Loan%20Estimate%20Advisory.pdf)
- For each DFPI path, confirm advertising, borrower terms, examination custody, and which annual, quarterly or audit submissions apply. Where authorities share a filing, verify its coverage using the entity, period and receipt. [DFPI Financing Law](https://dfpi.ca.gov/regulated-industries/california-financing-law/about-california-financing-law/) · [DFPI CRMLA reporting](https://dfpi.ca.gov/regulated-industries/california-residential-mortgage-lending-act/requirements-after-a-california-residential-mortgage-lender-and-or-servicer-license-has-been-issued/)

## Florida

- Review broker company, branch, principal loan originator and remote-work facts. Document which personnel and company changes need notice. Rehearse a location used to negotiate loans and a change of designated principal. Any lender or servicing activity needs its own authority and reporting review. [Florida OFR licensing](https://flofr.gov/regulation/apply-for-a-license/consumer-finance) · [OFR compliance tips](https://flofr.gov/divisions-offices/division-of-consumer-finance/finance-compliance-tips)
- The local amendment service records the effective date, license and event type, eligibility references for a principal originator, Registry filing evidence and separate regulator status. It shows a nominal 30-day post-change date for application information or principal originator changes. For control-person changes it records a reviewed advance or first-notice route and requires a reviewed calendar before calculating a two-business-day date. Counsel should confirm the route, cutoff and calendar, qualification evidence, and any prior approval for the exact event. Branch manager acceptance and other legal-event notices require separate review. The local service accepts bankruptcy notices using the date the action was instituted. It calculates 30 calendar days for brokers and 30 business days for lenders with a reviewed calendar. Staff entry for bankruptcy notices is pending. Confirm the Office of Financial Regulation submission channel, holiday or cutoff treatment and receipt evidence for the exact licensee. [Broker bankruptcy notice](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/494.004) | [Lender bankruptcy notice](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/494.0067) Open Compliance > Obligations, select the legal entity, and choose Florida license amendments to record or review the evidence. A fictional owner verified the form locally. Hosted acceptance is pending. [Amendment rule](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleno.asp?id=69V-40.099) · [Broker designation](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/494.0035) · [Lender designation](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/494.00665)
- Confirm how third-party fees are disclosed, collected, segregated, paid and reconciled for the brokerage. Rehearse an applicant payment followed by cancellation or refund, and capture the ledger and vendor evidence. Pair this with a solicitation review and a file the brokerage can retrieve for examination. [Florida Statutes Chapter 494](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0494/0494.html)

## Texas

- Identify SML mortgage company or banker authority and which personnel are sponsored. Review Chapter 342 separately if the entity proposes that activity. Rehearse the relevant company renewal and the filing route, including any state-specific MCR supplement for independent processing or underwriting. [SML origination FAQs](https://www.sml.texas.gov/mortgage-origination/faqs/) · [OCCC regulated lenders](https://occc.texas.gov/industry/regulated-lenders/)
- Have counsel confirm the applicable applicant notice, trigger, approved version and delivery evidence. Rehearse a dual-role transaction with written disclosure and consent, a website or ad with required identifiers and notices, and a complete broker file when the lender sends disclosures. [SML applicant notice](https://www.sml.texas.gov/download/mortgage-company-required-disclosure-form/) · [SML examination guide](https://www.sml.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/mortgage-compliance-guide-v12.pdf)
- In a local pilot workspace, open **Loan → Disclosures → Texas applicant notice and dual-role evidence**. Select the entity's Texas authority, record initial application receipt and document delivery for each applicant. For a dual role, add the written disclosure, consent, and first services in each role. Date-only same-day evidence needs review. Applications before November 23, 2024 go to the prior-rule review. Have counsel approve the initial receipt trigger, recipient and client scope, acceptable proof of electronic delivery, treatment of prior delivery, and the cutoff for dual-role consent. [Banker notice](https://www.sml.texas.gov/download/mortgage-banker-required-disclosure-form/) · [Adopted rules](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/pdf/backview/1115/1115adop.pdf) · [SML dual-role FAQ](https://www.sml.texas.gov/mortgage-origination/faqs/)

  The local notice service now calculates each deadline using 7 TAC §§56.5 and 57.5. It excludes the trigger date, counts ten calendar days and rolls the last day forward past weekends and legal holidays. The saved calendar must cover every date from the nominal deadline through the first eligible day. Each version retains the legal entity, custody and delivery evidence, deadline and review result. The owner opens Texas custody notices from Loan evidence & retention to record each company’s dates, custody reference and delivery proof. Local owner acceptance covered entry, validation, correction and original evidence after refresh. Signed-in hosted acceptance remains open. Rehearse a weekend followed by a holiday, separate cessation and receipt dates, an incomplete calendar and a late delivery. [SML rule update](https://www.sml.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2024MID-4-New-Rule-Update-Mortgage-Industry-Day-11-04-2024.pdf)

## New York

- Confirm broker registration, each operating location and originator authority. Review banker and servicer authority separately for any such activity. Rehearse the employment-status report and the current-year broker Volume of Operations Report, including when one NMLS filing can support another obligation. [Banking Law §590](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BNK/590) · [DFS mortgage company filings](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/apps_and_licensing/mortgage_companies)
- Have counsel confirm the operative pre-application and broker compensation disclosures, timing and agreement evidence, and interaction with federal compensation rules. Rehearse a compensation change, a broker ad that could be read as a lender offer, and retrieval of the complete transaction file. Verify current forms before any customer use. [DFS broker examination guide](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2021/04/mortgage_broker_guidebook.pdf) · [Regulation Z originator compensation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/36/)

## Illinois

- Confirm brokerage and originator authority, applicable annual financial filing, and evidence for any MCR substitution. Verify when a broker-only compilation is accepted and confirm the actual filing channel and deadline. [Illinois Residential Mortgage License Act](https://ilga.gov/legislation/ILCS/details?ActID=1196) · [IDFPR reporting forms](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/banks/resfin/forms/rfformsb.html)
- Rehearse company renewal against the license expiration and a request for more time to file an annual audit. Retain the regulator receipt, any written extension and verified license status. [IDFPR company renewal](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/banks/resfin/mbb.html) · [Illinois audit extension rule](https://witnessslips.ilga.gov/commission/jcar/admincode/038/038010500D04300R.html)
- The local Obligations service stores versioned audit evidence for a generated Illinois annual audit period. It tracks the reviewed original deadline, the written request and delivery date, the regulator's decision and the filing receipt. A documented grant supplies the extended date. Pending and denied requests use the original date. Counsel should confirm the actual IDFPR or NMLS deadline, the calendar used for the 15-day request window and the written decision for the exact licensee. In Compliance → Obligations, open the generated period and select Review Illinois audit evidence. The workflow passed local desktop and phone checks with fictional data. Hosted verification is pending. [Illinois audit extension rule](https://www.ilga.gov/commission/jcar/admincode/038/038010500D04300R.html)
- Rehearse the signed brokerage agreement when the signed completed application is the earlier event and when consideration comes first. Include the borrower copy, required disclosures, advertising identifiers, a terminated file, and the applicable loan log and retention evidence. [Illinois residential mortgage rules](https://www.ilga.gov/agencies/JCAR/EntirePart?titlepart=03801050)
- Review Anti-Predatory Lending Database scope, application and changed-term submissions, counseling and certificate responsibilities with counsel. Rehearse a covered county, a property outside the program area, a qualifying exemption, and a change in loan terms before closing. Retain submission and correction evidence in the scope approved for release. [Illinois APLD statute](https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076500770K70.htm) · [IDFPR APLD guidance](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/finlit101/apld.html)
- In a local pilot workspace, open **Loan → Disclosures → Illinois Anti-Predatory Lending Database**. Record the property county, the coverage or exemption basis, the initial submission, and each post-submission change or new commitment. Orbit compares submissions with documented deadlines under the 10- and five-business-day rules, tracks the database's counseling determination and the closing agent's certificate reference, and keeps earlier revisions. Enter evidence references for filings completed in the state database. Have counsel confirm the business-day calendar, exemption scope and certificate requirements, preclosing corrections under §72, and the separate submission duties of the title company or closing agent. [§70](https://my.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext?DocName=076500770K70) · [§72](https://www.ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076500770K72.htm) · [IDFPR exemption update](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/finlit101/sb1167.html)

The calendar shows a nominal December 31 date for the paths below. Check the actual renewal instructions and regulator status for each NMLS ID. If a saved state scope predates these requirements, save an updated scope version under **Research & gaps → State rollout** so the new source is available.

The planning service was tested with fictional scopes and calendar periods. These additions await hosted acceptance and entity-specific owner and lawyer review.

When only the research catalog changes, Orbit carries a recorded counsel disposition and its reference forward for each identical source. Control evidence stays on the earlier scope version for review. An existing obligation and its planned period keep their original basis through a catalog-only refresh when the authority, company, licenses and business facts still match. New or changed requirements need their own review.
