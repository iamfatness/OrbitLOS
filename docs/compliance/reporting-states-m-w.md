# Mortgage reporting research: Montana–Wyoming

Research date: 2026-11-11. Scope: 25 states, residential mortgage brokerage reporting, with lender/servicer distinctions where the reviewed sources establish them. **Preliminary research; not approved production rules or a finding of complete legal coverage.** User review and qualified lawyer approval are required before launch. Michigan is the first pilot; this document does not authorize another state's production rollout.

“Unresolved” means a research or applicability gap, not an exemption. No activity must never silently suppress an obligation. Sources are official regulator, legislature, or NMLS publications. Historic orders and guides are identified explicitly. Annual licensing renewals, financial statements, activity reports, and MCR are separate obligations unless a current authority explicitly substitutes one for another.

## Shared NMLS baseline and material version conflict

The [NMLS FV7 user guide](https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/mcr/licensing/mcr/topics/mcrUserGuide_start.html) describes quarterly RMLA and applicable state supplemental reporting within 45 days of calendar-quarter end (May 15, August 14, November 14, February 14). Broker FC is annual within 90 days of **calendar** year end; lender/servicer FC is quarterly. Applicability depends on licenses and activities. This is state licensing infrastructure, not a universal federal monthly return.

Several state guides below still describe annual FC using **fiscal** year end. Do not normalize these automatically. Determine whether the source refers to a separate state financial-statement obligation or superseded MCR instructions; retain both sources and obtain counsel/regulator resolution. Capture form version, reporting period, entity role, and any period-specific relief before generating a deadline. “MCR baseline” below references this section, not a finding that no additional state reports exist.

## Implementation consequences and review packet

1. Model an obligation per legal entity, license, jurisdiction, activity, form version and reporting period. “Broker” is not a uniform license label across states. Pending, denied, withdrawn, canceled, funded and purchased loans require separate status mappings; funded-loan counts alone cannot generate every report.
2. Maintain monthly close/reconciliation tasks independently of statutory filing cadence. Nevada provides a verified monthly example; that does not make MCR, HMDA or every state's reporting monthly.
3. Create distinct report families for MCR RMLA, FC, SSSF, state activity logs, state annual returns, CPA financial statements, deposit verification and renewal reports. Link supporting documents, snapshot the underlying loan ledger, reconcile totals, preserve corrections and retain submission receipts.
4. Unknown nil-return, deadline adjustment, timezone, threshold, exemption and submission-method fields remain explicitly unresolved. They must surface as coverage gaps, not produce a green compliance status. Holiday adjustment must be authority-specific: New Hampshire's cited statute expressly moves weekend/state/federal-holiday deadlines to the next business day and uses commissioner receipt; do not generalize that rule to other jurisdictions.
5. Counsel must resolve MCR substitution questions (notably Oregon/Vermont), calendar/fiscal FC conflicts, role overlap and transitional relief. Lock approved rules to effective periods; a source with a 2025 return and 2026 deadline does not establish a 2027 deadline.
6. Before enabling production for any state, obtain user review followed by lawyer approval of applicability, current authority, deadlines, nil activity, form schema and generated example. Test with fictional records until that gate passes. None of these rows currently satisfies the complete approval gate.

## Source-specific detail worth preserving

- Nevada's [MLD Form 502 instructions](https://mld.nv.gov/uploadedfiles/mldnvgov/content/Industry/General_Forms_and_Correspondence/MLDForm502InstructionsforFilingMonthlyActivityReportForm-1-8-2018.pdf) distinguish the **MLD license number from NMLS ID** and use the funding month. This 2018 document is schema evidence to reconcile against the latest workbook, not permission to ship the old template.
- New Hampshire's separate annual statement includes GAAP statements/notes and an officer certification when unaudited. Treat it as a documentary filing, not merely numeric FC fields.
- New York DFS accepts NMLS MCR in place of manual Quarterly Commitment Data; that does not remove annual VOOR. Servicer VOSR is separate and quarterly.
- Rhode Island requires licensee financials; consolidated statements need a standalone licensee breakdown. This is an upload/evidence validation requirement beyond a populated FC form.
- South Carolina's no-activity test concerns credit decisions, including decisions by a lender on a broker's submissions. Zero funded loans alone is insufficient evidence for a no-activity attestation.
- Washington's [consumer loan assessment page](https://dfi.wa.gov/consumer-loan-companies/annual-assessments) gives March 1, 2027 for the 2026 assessment. This is evidence for the consumer-loan license regime, not a broker-only requirement.

Coverage: all 25 assigned states have an entry and an official source lead. Depth is uneven and explicitly marked; this is a research register and release-blocker inventory, not a claim that every reporting obligation has been discovered or legally validated.
