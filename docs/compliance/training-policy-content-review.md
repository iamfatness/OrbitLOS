# CS4 / CS5 training and policy content review

Research date: 2026-11-11. **Original proposed curriculum; draft, not legal-approved.** Michigan is the pilot. The user reviews first; a lawyer must review before launch. This document supports engineering content readiness and does not certify a brokerage, course, employee, policy, or platform as compliant. No commercial course or competitor policy text was copied.

## What the training calendar means

The twelve slots below are a proposed monthly internal assignment cycle, not twelve statutory monthly training mandates. Initial training belongs before a person undertakes the relevant work; onboarding and corrective/change-triggered assignments must not wait for the topic's calendar month. Compliance management sets additional frequency using the entity's risks, work, monitoring findings and applicable law.

| Authority / requirement | Actual cadence and people | Product implication / required reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Michigan MLO continuing education, MCL493.147 | Licensed MLOs: at least eight approved hours annually, including three federal law, two ethics, two nontraditional mortgage lending. Course and provider must be NMLS approved; same course cannot satisfy the same or successive years. [Michigan statute](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-493-147) | Track separately from employee learning. Internal completion earns **no NMLS CE credit** without independent course/provider approval and verified credit reporting. Licensing specialist and lawyer verify individual applicability. |
| Michigan renewal instructions | CE must precede renewal request; DIFS identifies an exception for MLOs completing prelicensure education in the year licensed. [DIFS licensing](https://www.michigan.gov/difs/industry/licensing-cf/mort-loan-org) | Do not equate twelve internal lessons with renewal eligibility. Store external provider/course/year/completion and verified NMLS evidence; account for exceptions through review. |
| AML program training, 31 CFR1029.210(b)(3) | Ongoing training of appropriate persons on responsibilities under the program; direct training or competent-third-party training relevant to offered products/services. No fixed monthly interval established here. [FinCEN final rule](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/shared/1506-AB02_RMLO_Final_Rule.pdf) | AML officer and lawyer confirm current rule, covered entity and risk-based cadence. April 2026 AML proposal is a proposal, not an activated requirement; recheck final-rule status before publishing. [2026 proposal](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-04-10/html/2026-07033.htm) |
| FTC Safeguards Rule, 16 CFR314.4(e) | Personnel security-awareness training updated to reflect identified risks; qualified security personnel maintain relevant knowledge. FTC identifies mortgage brokers among potentially covered financial institutions. [FTC guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftc-safeguards-rule-what-your-business-needs-know) | Qualified Individual/security owner and lawyer validate scope, exemptions and curriculum. Internal monthly microlearning is a proposed control, not the rule's prescribed interval. |
| Regulation Z, 12 CFR1026.36(f)(3)(iii) | Periodic training for the specified individual originators who are neither required to be licensed nor licensed under SAFE implementing law; scope must be determined before assignment. [CFPB current rule](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/36/) | Do not describe this subsection as a universal monthly requirement or substitute it for licensed-MLO CE. Counsel distinguishes employee categories and product scope. |
| Compliance management supervision | CFPB review framework examines the institution's compliance management system, including its compliance program and management oversight. It is supervisory guidance, not a twelve-course statute. [CFPB examination procedures](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervision-examinations/compliance-management-review-examination-procedures/) | Compliance owner chooses role-based internal learning and policy governance; counsel determines the entity's supervisory context. |

All other topic slots below teach underlying duties and recommended operational controls. Their cited regulations do not, merely by being cited, establish a separate annual or monthly course obligation.

## Twelve original draft topic outlines

Suggested slot labels are cycle positions, not mandatory calendar dates. Each proposed lesson needs authored instructional text, an original assessment and approved answer rationale before release; outlines alone cannot count as completed training.

|---|---|---|---|
| T01 / 1 — Licensing and permitted work | MLOs, processors, managers; licensing lead teaches, Michigan lawyer reviews | Distinguish company license, individual license and clerical work; check sponsorship and authority before work; route changed roles. Exercise: decide who must review a fictional new hire's permissions before borrower contact. | Licensing/role authorization and renewal procedure. [DIFS MLO licensing](https://www.michigan.gov/difs/industry/licensing-cf/mort-loan-org), [Michigan CE statute](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-493-147). |

## CS5 policy review and publication gates

Every policy named above begins as a draft template. The brokerage must supply actual owners, systems, escalation contacts, license scope, products, authority limits and document retention choices. Empty organization-specific placeholders block publication. Acknowledging a policy does not prove a control was performed.

| Gate | Required evidence | Fail condition |
|---|---|---|
| Source/applicability | Authority URL, provision, retrieved/effective dates, covered role/entity/activity, unresolved questions | Missing scope, stale authority, proposal treated as operative law, or reliance on another license type |
| Content rights | Original author/provenance; rights for any third-party excerpt, image, assessment or licensed course; permission for distribution and adaptation | Vendor content copied without permission; “publicly accessible” treated as a license |
| Subject expertise | Named qualified content reviewer: AML officer, security Qualified Individual, CPA/reporting lead, licensing or fair-lending specialist as relevant | Generic administrator approval presented as specialist review |
| User review | User records workflow/content review for the specific version | Review inferred from prior general approval |
| Lawyer review | Identified lawyer approves exact jurisdiction/entity scope and version; resolves open legal questions | User approval substituted for lawyer approval; changes silently inherit old sign-off |
| Employer adoption | Authorized brokerage owner adopts policy, names accountable roles and effective date | Platform template presented as adopted company policy |
| Accessibility/content QA | Keyboard and screen-reader checks, captions/transcript where needed, accessible assessment and feedback, approved answer rationale | Completion requires inaccessible video, drag-only interaction, color-only meaning or unsupported timing barriers |
| Controlled publication | Immutable approved version, effective date, audience rules, rollback/supersession link, reassignment decision | Unapproved or superseded material assigned as current |

Legal review must also set cadence for policy review and training evidence retention. A proposed annual policy review plus event-triggered updates is an internal operating choice until mapped to a particular requirement. Do not label it a universal law. Regulatory changes, new products, incidents, complaints and audit findings should trigger a documented impact review.

## CS4 accessible assessment and completion evidence

Proposed baseline: each lesson has a brief knowledge check, one realistic application scenario, feedback explaining each answer, and a documented remediation path. A suggested 80% passing score is an employer design choice; the content owner and lawyer must approve scoring and any critical questions before publication. No statutory pass score is asserted. Critical mistakes require remediation and reattempt under the adopted policy, not silent completion.

Provide equivalent text for audio/video, captions, programmatic labels, logical reading/focus order, keyboard-operable choices, readable feedback and save/resume. Permit reasonable accommodations and alternative equivalent assessments without exposing medical details in routine training exports. Any timed or identity-verification requirement must be justified for its specific program; ordinary internal lessons should not inherit CE-provider restrictions automatically.

Persist learner/organization identity, role and assignment rationale, assigned/due dates, content/version hash, source/reviewer metadata, attempts, answers or minimized scoring evidence, feedback/remediation, attestation, completion timestamp and actor. Retain immutable history when lessons change. Separate states: assigned, in progress, submitted, passed, remediation required, overdue, waived with approved reason, and superseded. A waiver is not a pass; an administrator must not fabricate learner activity.

External NMLS CE evidence lives in a distinct record with provider/course approval identifiers, course year, approved hours/categories and verified transcript/credit evidence. Internal certificates should say **internal training completion; not NMLS CE credit or regulatory certification**. Do not issue regulator-looking badges or infer credit from similar topics.

Use fictional scenarios and synthetic borrower information. Restrict assessment answers, personnel records and accommodations by role; keep detailed SAR/investigation examples out of general learner evidence. Set retention and legal holds after counsel review rather than inheriting a mortgage-file retention number blindly.

## Engineering readiness acceptance

- All twelve outlines remain draft until full lesson, assessment and policy content have passed the applicable gates.
- Monthly internal assignments, legally required ongoing/periodic training, and external annual CE are different obligation types with independent evidence.
- Roles and jurisdictions determine assignments; no universal course completion can clear every compliance risk.
- Missing lawyer approval, rights clearance, required expertise, accessibility QA or employer adoption is visible as a blocking content gap.
- Tests must show that version changes reopen relevant reviews, overdue assignments remain visible, inactive users preserve historical evidence, tenant data remain isolated, and a completed internal course cannot mark NMLS CE satisfied.
- Publication readiness is not proof of deployed functionality, content adoption or launch authorization. Michigan launch remains contingent on the user's review and lawyer approval.
