## Michigan first-mortgage charge evidence

In **Disclosures → TRID application and delivery evidence**, choose **Record Michigan charge review**. Record the charge key, classification, party and lien scope, amount charged, payment total and collection count from the supporting records. For an actual expense, include the expense amount and supporting reference. For a rate guarantee, include the written terms, original expiration, and any written extension agreed to by all parties. An incomplete record remains open for review. A second active entry for the same charge key is rejected. Withdraw the earlier entry to record a correction and reopen it in Evidence history if needed.

The review flags payment totals above the assessed charge, multiple collections that need reconciliation, expense evidence gaps and incomplete guarantee or extension evidence. The pilot uses staff-entered figures and scope. Counsel needs to confirm who assessed or accepted the charge, which costs were reasonable and necessary, how to treat repeated or split collections, and whether the writing and any extension satisfy the applicable requirements. The Michigan checks sit alongside the separate federal TRID fee and timing reviews. Regulatory use remains subject to owner and lawyer review. [Michigan Legislature, MCL 445.1673](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-445-1673)

## Michigan application booklet

In **Disclosures → TRID application and delivery evidence**, choose **Record Michigan booklet review**. Record the Michigan application event and channel for the reviewed applicant. When delivery is documented, record the provider, method, exact booklet version and evidence. The result shows recorded order or missing timing. A same-day record without both local times stays unresolved. Corrections preserve the original evidence. This workflow keeps the state application event independent of the six federal TRID application elements.

Counsel should determine which borrower and loan types are covered, what constitutes an application for the entity and channel, which booklet is operative, and whether the delivery proof and timing satisfy Michigan and any applicable federal requirement. DIFS guidance addresses refinances, web and telephone applications, and when a lender can use evidence of a broker's delivery. The pilot records staff-entered evidence for one applicant. [Michigan Legislature, MCL 445.1636](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-445-1636) · [DIFS Consumer Mortgage Protection Act FAQ](https://www.michigan.gov/difs/news-and-outreach/faq/consumer-finance/consumer-mort-protection-act)

## Covered loan practices

Michigan's Consumer Mortgage Protection Act defines coverage by lien or land contract, Michigan principal dwelling, number of families, purpose and product. The definition excludes acquisition of the borrower's principal dwelling, reverse mortgages, qualifying open-end credit and transactions whose proceeds are primarily for purposes other than personal, family or household use. DIFS explains that different sections refer to different parties and gives its opinion on broker responsibilities. Counsel should confirm the entity's role and the covered transactions before turning any of these reviews into automated decisions. [Michigan Legislature, MCL 445.1632](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=MCL-445-1632) · [DIFS Consumer Mortgage Protection Act FAQ](https://www.michigan.gov/difs/news-and-outreach/faq/consumer-finance/consumer-mort-protection-act)

The Michigan **Loan terms and disclosures** catalog has separate reviews for services actually provided and third-party charge evidence, application and signed-note integrity, appraisal independence, financed single-premium credit insurance, and short-term payment schedules. For internal rehearsal, exercise a canceled service, an unsupported application change, a disputed valuation communication, a financed premium, and a term below five years with residual principal. Include a documented bridge scenario below one year and one exactly at one year so counsel can review the exception boundary. Counsel should assign the brokerage and wholesale lender responsibilities and review federal overlap before regulatory use. [Michigan Legislature, Consumer Mortgage Protection Act §§4–5](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/pdf/mcl-Act-660-of-2002.pdf)

The local compliance service now saves versioned evidence for these five requirements on accessible Michigan loans. It stores the reviewer's scope and evidence references, reports gaps and concerns separately by catalog requirement, and preserves prior versions on correction. An assigned processor can read the record. The assigned loan officer or admin can save while the loan is active. Open **Loan → Disclosures → Michigan loan practices evidence**, available for Michigan pilot files before 1003 submission. Staff can record scope and findings, save a corrected version, and reopen the original evidence. A fictional owner and assigned processor verified the workflow in the local built Worker. Production promotion is on hold and counsel review is pending.

The payment-schedule review uses the recorded contractual term start and maturity, the reviewer's assessment of amortization through regular payments, and documented acquisition or construction bridge purpose for the intended principal dwelling. Exactly five years falls outside the short-term comparison. Exactly one year does not satisfy the under-one-year bridge condition. A February 29 anniversary that cannot be resolved by the recorded dates stays open. Counsel should confirm the governing start date and anniversary treatment, how the scope and bridge facts apply, and the evidence of amortization. The review surfaces evidence for follow-up and never authorizes a loan or a closing.
