# Michigan pilot counsel review

Complete one review for the exact legal entity and version of the scope saved under **Compliance → Research & gaps → State rollout → Michigan**. The current working assumption for rehearsal is a brokerage handling first-lien closed-end residential loans with a wholesale lender. Confirm the assumption, identify excluded scenarios, and open a separate scope review for any secondary lien, lending, servicing, open-end, reverse, or other product activity proposed for launch.

## Brokerage evidence for counsel

- Legal entity name, NMLS company ID, each Michigan license or registration number, approved trade names and authority dates. Include the company and branch or remote-office arrangement, Michigan resident agent and business authority evidence. Attach current NMLS renewal instructions, any applicable bond, and current regulator status. [DIFS mortgage licensing](https://www.michigan.gov/difs/industry/licensing-cf/mortgage) · [DIFS office and agent guidance](https://www.michigan.gov/difs/news-and-outreach/faq/consumer-finance/mortgage-compliance2)
- Proposed staff roster, permitted activity, license and sponsor evidence for each originator, and records for any claimed exemption or Temporary Authority. Include how the brokerage will recheck authority at assignment and before regulated actions. [MCL 493.135](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-493-135)
- Product and lien list, company fiscal year-end and reporting owner, broker and lender responsibility for disclosures, which party collects each fee, evidence of disclosures and receipt, retention and examination custody, and marketing channels in use.

For each decision, capture the requirement key, exact authority and effective date, applicable products and parties, any exception and evidence, the control or operating procedure, and the counsel disposition with its date and reference. Store the reference with the scope and retain privileged advice outside Orbit. Record any work that remains open with an owner and next review date. Link the exact release and eligible workspace after release acceptance. The reviewer and lawyer still need to complete these decisions for the real brokerage.
