# Federal compliance requirements for OrbitLOS

Research date: **2026-11-10**. Scope: a multi-tenant LOS/CRM supporting wholesale mortgage brokers, residential applications, realtor referrals, lead campaigns, credit/AUS handoffs and closing coordination. This is an engineering requirements baseline, not a legal opinion, exhaustive inventory or finding that Orbit or a brokerage complies. The source-linked [25-family register](federal-sources.json) supplies applicability, requirements, current gaps and required evidence for the platform explorer. Each family still needs an accountable brokerage compliance owner and counsel-approved applicability before production enforcement.

## Determine who must do what

The regulated brokerage, individual originator, wholesale creditor, settlement agent and Orbit as technology/service provider have different duties. Store responsibility per obligation and lender agreement; outsourcing performance does not necessarily transfer legal responsibility. Intake must capture property and borrower locations, purpose, occupancy, lien, units, borrower/entity type, product, entity/branch/officer authority, creditor and meaningful application timestamps. Missing facts should produce **review required**, never an inferred exemption.

Business-purpose treatment depends on actual facts, not the product name. DSCR and bank-statement loans need documented classification. A TILA business-purpose exemption does not establish exemptions from ECOA, FCRA, licensing, sanctions, privacy or state laws. The rental-property commentary and mixed-purpose factors need a qualified determination. [Regulation Z §1026.3](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/3/).

## Disclosure, pricing and closing controls

| Register | Operational requirement and evidence | Required acceptance test |
|---|---|---|
| FED-02 | For covered TRID applications, capture receipt of name, income, SSN to obtain credit, property address, estimated property value and loan amount sought. LE generally must be delivered/mailed within three business days, with separate pre-consummation timing. Do not wait for a signed 1003 or verification documents to start the clock. CD generally must be received at least three business days before consummation. Distinguish creditor-business-day and statutory-business-day definitions. Track receipt presumptions, changed circumstances, fee tolerances/cures, permissible early credit-report fee and intent to proceed. | Incrementally receiving the sixth item starts the correct clock; weekends/holidays, electronic receipt, corrected APR/product/prepayment terms, stale package and missing lender receipt all exercise reviewed rules. |
| FED-03 | Determine rescission eligibility independently. The ordinary period ends at midnight on the third business day after the last relevant triggering event; nondelivery can extend rights. Every entitled owner matters, including some nonborrower owners. | Funding is held for missing notice/recipient or unexpired period; purchase and valid exception cases use reviewed outcomes, not an automatic universal delay. |
| FED-04–06 | Separate HOEPA, HPML and ATR/QM tests. Store effective-date thresholds, APOR evidence, points/fee inclusion, loan features and creditor/product exceptions. Keep lender determination and underlying verified underwriting records. | A fee/term change invalidates old calculations; unknown threshold or unsupported product blocks automated approval. |
| FED-07 | Compensation plans, lender/borrower payer, steering and required originator identification must be reviewed before quotes and payments. | Terms-based compensation or incompatible payer configurations route to review; quote comparison keeps the actual options and selection rationale. |

Sources: [TRID timing and fees](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/19/), [rescission](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/23/), [HOEPA](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/32/), [HPML](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/35/), [ATR/QM](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/43/), [originator compensation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/36/). The JSON and this table describe one combined control scope; they are not independent legal conclusions.

RESPA referral controls must cover realtor relationships, co-marketing, purchased leads, referral incentives and settlement-service payments. Actual services and reasonable compensation require review; labeling a fee “marketing” proves nothing. Affiliated arrangements need their own disclosure and exception analysis, including restrictions on required use. [§1024.14](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/14/), [§1024.15](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/15/).

Include additional product-specific notices in the approved document-provider matrix: home-loan toolkit/booklet and counseling-list requirements where applicable, ARM, HELOC, reverse-mortgage, servicing-transfer and government-program requirements. These are **not implemented or exhaustively researched here**. Do not enable a new product merely because its generic 1003 can be stored. Servicing, foreclosure, loan modification, warehouse/correspondent lending, FHA/VA/USDA participation, flood insurance and military borrower protections require separate scope assessment before those activities are supported.

## Application decisions and reporting

| Register | Rule-specific design | Required evidence/test |
|---|---|---|
| FED-09 | Reg B generally requires action notification within 30 days after receiving a completed application; other incomplete, existing-credit and counteroffer paths have distinct triggers. Determine who is a creditor for each provision. Business credit has different notification provisions. | Never turn a denial into a withdrawn/lost lead to suppress a notice. Keep truthful reasons, decision timestamp, notice issuer/content/delivery and incomplete-application follow-up. |
| FED-10 | Protect against unlawful discriminatory treatment across marketing, routing, pricing and decisions. Segregate monitoring data from ordinary underwriting use. FHA protections require a separate analysis from ECOA. | Review segment/routing criteria, accessibility and pricing overrides; retain changes, complaints and investigations. Do not claim statistical monitoring proves compliance. |
| FED-11 | For covered first-lien dwelling applications, provide appraisal-right notice within three business days and copies promptly upon completion or three business days before consummation/account opening, whichever is earlier, subject to applicable waiver rules. | Missing, revised and late appraisals and timing-only waivers are separate states; denial does not automatically eliminate copy duties. |
| FED-12 | Determine institutional and transaction HMDA coverage annually. Nondepository volume tests include 25 closed-end or 200 open-end originations in each of the two preceding years plus other conditions. Who makes the credit decision matters in broker arrangements. | HMDA LAR is distinct from NMLS Mortgage Call Report. Retain coverage analysis, lender attribution, data lineage, edit/filing evidence and controlled demographic access. |

Sources: [Reg B notifications](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/9/), [current Reg B](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/), [Fair Housing Act](https://www.justice.gov/crt/fair-housing-act-1), [valuation copies](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/14/), [HMDA definitions](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1003/2/).

**Current-rule review:** CFPB reports April 22, 2026 amendments concerning disparate impact, discouragement and special-purpose credit programs. Do not reuse an older interpretation as current without checking effective text and litigation. The May 1, 2026 section 1071 reconsideration changes coverage/data requirements and moves compliance to January 1, 2028 according to the official summary. Business-purpose/DSCR expansion needs a fresh coverage assessment; this is not a universal broker filing obligation. [Reg B amendment](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/final-rules/equal-credit-opportunity-act-regulation-b/), [1071 rulemaking](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/1071-rule/).

## Credit, lead acquisition and communications

For FED-13, a report order must preserve permissible purpose and provider authorization, report/borrower identity and notice responsibility. A consumer authorization checkbox alone does not prove every use is permissible. Adverse action based on report information, credit scores and risk-based pricing carry separate notice requirements/exceptions. A mortgage-score exception notice is not a generic substitute for every adverse-action notice. [FTC credit-decision guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/using-consumer-reports-credit-decisions-what-know-about-adverse-action-risk-based-pricing-notices).

For FED-14, Public Law 119-36 restricts furnishing mortgage inquiry-triggered prescreened reports subject to specified authorization/relationship exceptions; it took effect 180 days after its September 5, 2025 enactment. Vendor contracts and CSV columns cannot establish the exception alone. Require acquisition source/date, report provenance and supporting authorization/qualifying relationship; quarantine unknown imported trigger leads. The restriction should not be misrepresented as a blanket ban on all mortgage marketing. [Enacted law](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ36/pdf/PLAW-119publ36.pdf).

For FED-19–21, establish a pre-contact policy service shared by manual call queues, automated sends, retries and callbacks. Evaluate seller, channel, marketing versus transaction purpose, dialing/voice technology, phone type, recipient-local time, national/state/internal suppression, applicable consent and source evidence. Consent, E-SIGN consent, privacy consent and credit authorization are separate records. Recording an opt-out must cancel queued outreach and prevent replay from restoring it. Requested callbacks need specific permission context; they are not blanket permission for every campaign. [FCC delivery restrictions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200), [FTC TSR guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule), [CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).

FCC rules/waivers are changing: a September 2026 FCC circulated document cites a January 2026 order extending the cross-category “revoke all” provision waiver through January 31, 2027. The cited order could not be retrieved directly during this research, and a circulated proposal is not a final adopted rule. **Current-order verification remains open**; do not encode the older April 2026 date or a proposal as binding law. Immediate broad suppression can be a deliberate product policy, labeled separately from a statutory minimum. [FCC circulated document and waiver references](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-424844A1.pdf).

Advertising also needs APR/trigger-term review, accurate rate/availability representations, and applicable mortgage advertising/UDAP/UDAAP requirements. A saved quote is not automatically an approved advertisement. Archive exact published content and approved assumptions. [Reg Z advertising](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/24/). MAP/Reg N and broader unfair/deceptive practice coverage need further rule-level legal mapping.

## Privacy, security, licensing and financial crime

| Register | Required implementation and operating evidence |
|---|---|
| FED-15 | Entity/branch/individual license authority and sponsorship need verified state-specific records and expiry controls. Staff membership is separate from authority to originate. [SAFE Regulation H](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1008/). |
| FED-16 | Maintain notice versions/delivery, sharing-purpose inventory and applicable opt-out/exception evidence. A website privacy policy does not automatically satisfy consumer financial privacy notices. [Regulation P](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1016/). |
| FED-17 | Broker operational security program and Orbit vendor controls must include accountable oversight, risk assessment, access/MFA/encryption, testing, service-provider review, incident response and applicable reports. FTC notification analysis includes unauthorized acquisition of unencrypted information involving at least 500 consumers; notification is as soon as possible and no later than 30 days after discovery. State breach duties are additional. [FTC Safeguards guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftc-safeguards-rule-what-your-business-needs-know). |
| FED-18 | Preserve affirmative consumer electronic consent, required disclosures, demonstrated access, withdrawal/paper procedures and accurate accessible records. Test each signer and superseded package; provider callbacks must match actual artifacts. [E-SIGN §7001](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap96-subchapI-sec7001.htm). |
| FED-22 | Covered RMLOs need an AML program with officer, training and independent testing. SAR evaluation includes qualifying suspicious transactions aggregating at least $5,000; ordinary filing deadline is 30 calendar days after initial detection, with limited extension to 60 if no suspect is identified. Retain SAR/supporting records five years. SAR existence/content must never reach borrower/realtor portals, routine loan exports or ordinary activity. [31 CFR Part 1029](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1029). |
| FED-23 | Preserve sanctions screening inputs/list version and human potential-match resolution; relevant ownership and program restrictions matter beyond exact-name matches. OFAC coverage is not confined to banks. [OFAC overview](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/topic/1501). |
| FED-25 | Test keyboard, screen reader, errors, authentication, PDF/signing and accommodation paths. WCAG can be the engineering target; do not falsely present the public-entity Title II deadline as automatically controlling a private brokerage. [DOJ web accessibility guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/). |

Non-financed real-estate transfer reporting is a distinct regime; do not assume it replaces RMLO AML/SAR obligations or applies to every brokered financed loan. Any settlement/reporting-person role requires current-rule and litigation review before enabling that workflow. [FinCEN real-estate FAQs](https://www.fincen.gov/rre-faqs).

## Retention and deletion design

Use a per-record schedule with legal basis, responsible entity, trigger, minimum duration, hold and destruction decision. Reg Z has a general two-year period and important special rules: relevant LE evidence generally three years, completed CD and related documents five years after consummation, ATR evidence three years and covered originator-compensation records three years. Do not apply one universal mortgage duration. [§1026.25](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/25/).

Reg B generally requires consumer application/action records for 25 months; business-credit rules and investigations have different provisions. [§1002.12](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/12/). Current TSR recordkeeping generally requires five years, with record-specific content and exceptions; older two-year summaries are insufficient. [16 CFR §310.5](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.5).

Apply the approved maximum overlapping obligation to each artifact; preserve legal holds across database rows, uploaded files, backups, provider artifacts and exports. “Superseded” must not imply “safe to delete.” Privacy deletion requests need verified identity and applicable retention exceptions with a recorded disposition.

## Verified repository observations and release gates

Inspection found manual `disclosureStatuses` and `disclosureDate` in `src/lib/model.ts`, with updates in `src/lib/commands.ts`. `src/lib/credit-aus-model.ts` and `mock-credit-aus.ts` are explicitly mock services. `lead-inbox-model.ts` validates a generic consent boolean. `call-campaign-model.ts` excludes opted-out/suppressed leads. These are useful foundations, not statutory evidence or a completed compliance engine. Existing docs explicitly describe provider/disclosure work as incomplete (`docs/lendingpad-gap-spec.md`, LP05; `docs/arive-feature-parity-spec.md`, F05/F11/F16). No full code/security audit was performed in this research.

Before real production activity, each enabled family needs approved applicability and responsibility, versioned rule and effective dates, implemented server-side control, retained evidence, adverse-path tests and operational owner. Unknown facts, stale source review, missing provider evidence and unsupported products should remain visible blockers. Human waiver must identify a lawful exception and reviewer; an administrator toggle cannot waive a legal requirement.

The rules register also needs scheduled legal maintenance for amendments, injunctions, thresholds, regulator forms, provider changes and state overlays. No automated source refresh or counsel review has been completed by creating these files.
